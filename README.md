# 🏆 Tournament Arena — Roguelike Football Clash

Standalone tactical Roguelike Tournament game with non-RNG observation mechanics and dynamic card drafting synergies.

## 🌟 Key Features
- **Zero RNG**: Goal outcomes are 100% skill-based on goalkeeper telegraph stance reading.
- **Roguelike Card Drafting**: Draft powerful Buffs & Cursed Modifier cards after winning stages.
- **Power & Accuracy Meter**: Time your strikes in the gold critical zone for score multipliers and unstoppable shot speed.
- **3-Tier Stage Progression**: Quarter-Finals -> Semi-Finals -> Grand Finals vs Supreme King Jeff.
- **Synthesizer Web Audio API**: Full procedural sound effects and audio feedback.
